Compliance & Ethics

Privacy Notice.

Effective Date: October 2026 • Nagpur, Maharashtra, India

1. Introduction

Wisdom Technosoft Pvt Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") provides software development, telecalling, and data management services. We are committed to transparency, ethical data handling, and respecting your privacy.

This short notice plainly outlines what information is gathered through this website, why we gather it, and how it is protected.

Core Principle: We do not track you across the web, we do not sell your personal data, and we collect only what you choose to give us to start a conversation.

2. What We Collect and Why

We collect personal information solely when you voluntarily submit it through our website contact form or directly communicate with us via phone, email, or WhatsApp. This includes:

  • Full Name: To address you properly in our correspondence.
  • Email Address: To send you project scopes, proposals, and responses to your inquiries.
  • Phone / WhatsApp Number: To connect for scheduled consultations or telephonic discussions if requested.
  • Service of Interest & Project Notes: To understand your business requirements and evaluate how our software, telecalling, or data teams can assist.

3. Zero Tracking & Analytics at Launch

At launch, this website operates with no tracking cookies, no cross-site surveillance beacons, and no third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics or advertising pixels).

If privacy-preserving, aggregated website metrics are ever adopted in the future, we will choose a privacy-first, cookieless solution (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Plausible) and update this notice accordingly.

4. Third-Party Scripts & Embeds

We intentionally do not embed unnecessary third-party scripts, advertising widgets, social media trackers, or iframes. Our physical office map link points directly to Google Maps in a standard link, ensuring external platforms cannot track your browsing session on our site.

5. Data Retention & Sharing

Information provided through our contact form is delivered directly to our designated business inbox. We retain inquiries only for legitimate business purposes—specifically, to communicate with you regarding your service request.

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade client information to third-party brokers, marketing lists, or advertising platforms.
